First, load the C64 system ROMs

The C64 can't switch on without the ROM chips Commodore built into it. They are still under copyright, so they aren't included here: choose or drop your own ROM image files. They stay in this browser and are never sent anywhere. The browser remembers them, so you only need to do this once.

  • BASIC ROM Required

    Commodore BASIC V2, chip 901226-01. Exactly 8 KB (8,192 bytes).

    Often named basic.rom, basic, basic-901226-01.bin or basic.901226-01.bin

  • KERNAL ROM Required

    The operating system, chip 901227-03 (or the older -02 / -01). Exactly 8 KB (8,192 bytes).

    Often named kernal.rom, kernal, kernal-901227-03.bin or kernal.901227-03.bin

  • Character ROM Required

    The screen font, chip 901225-01. Exactly 4 KB (4,096 bytes).

    Often named chargen.rom, chargen, chargen-901225-01.bin or characters.901225-01.bin

  • 1541 disk drive ROM Recommended

    The 1541 drive's DOS (325302-01 + 901229-05, or the 1541-II's 251968-03) as one file. Exactly 16 KB (16,384 bytes). Without it only the fast virtual drive is available, and most games with fast loaders won't load.

    Often named dos1541.rom, dos1541, dos1541-325302-01+901229-05.bin or 1541-II.251968-03.bin

Pick several files at once, or a .zip containing them. The VICE emulator keeps them in its C64 and DRIVES folders.
